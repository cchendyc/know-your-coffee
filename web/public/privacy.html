Know Your Coffee

Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

Know Your Coffee is a community guide to specialty coffee shops, available at knowyourthings.top and as an iOS app. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account info. When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. When you sign in with a phone number or email code, we store that phone number or email address.
  • Content you post. Shop reports, photos, notes, saved shops, and been-to marks are stored with your account and shown to other users alongside your name and picture.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't track you across other sites or apps.
  • We don't request access to your contacts, location history, or files.

Third-party services

Sign-in is handled by Google and Apple; their handling of your credentials is covered by their own policies. Shop data and photos come from Google Places. Our database is hosted on Neon (Postgres) and the API on Render.

Sessions

After sign-in we store a session token on your device (browser storage on the web, Keychain on iOS) so you stay signed in. Sessions expire after 30 days. Signing out deletes the token.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything attached to it, open an issue at github.com/cchendyc/know-your-coffee or contact the maintainer. We'll remove your account, reports, and photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be noted in the app.
